Integrabot is a platform for building **AI Agents for customer service on the official WhatsApp** (Meta's WhatsApp
Business Platform, Cloud API). The Agent answers your customers using your business information, moves each
conversation to the next step and hands it to your team when a person is needed.

If you don't want to, or can't yet, connect WhatsApp, you can put the Agent on your own website with the **site
widget**.
